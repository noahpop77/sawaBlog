Just finished reading the first trilogy of the Red Rising series. This includes Red Rising, Golden Son, and Morning Star.

I do not think its the most novel story ever written but what I do think is that it does an excellent job at getting you hooked and invested in the characters ("wakey wakey goblin"). It also keeps you on your toes as you realize that Darrow isn't always reliable in his role as the narrator of his own story and surprises can come about based off the character either deluding himself or not noticing details.

I also really appreciate the transition Darrow went through internally from his vengeful youth to an adulthood where his focus is less on tearing everything down but more so on what he will have to rebuild.

Loved the book, great read. BREAK THE CHAINS!

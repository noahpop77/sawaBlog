With the advent of AI in the recent years becoming significantly more useful compared to early generation models it has affected the landscape of how people want to work and how they actually work. This is obvious. I hate that AI has been injected into every part of tech, similarly to my misgivings of the great migration to the cloud.

An observation that I have come across however is that, given the transformer nature of AI and how it strives for the 50th percentile of whatever it makes, it provides everyone with the ability to produce something mediocre. Compounding with the fact that people caring less and less about quality software (every piece of software is perma broken and nobody cares it seems).

With that said, the HOW something is made is seen as "solved" by AI (which I disagree with), but the part that makes quite a bit more sense is that there is now more emphasis on the WHY something is made.
